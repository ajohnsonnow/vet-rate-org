## 📊 Calculate Your Rating

### 🧮 Tactical Calculator

**What it does:** Calculates your combined VA disability rating using official VA Math, including bilateral factors and 2026 compensation rates.

**How to access:** Tools → Calculate Your Rating → Tactical Calculator

**Features:**
- ✅ Official VA Math formula (not simple addition!)
- ✅ Bilateral factor automatic detection
- ✅ 2026 monthly compensation with dependents
- ✅ "What-If" scenarios - see how adding conditions changes your rating
- ✅ Gap analysis - shows how close you are to the next tier
- ✅ Save your ratings with "My Ratings" feature

**Example:**
```
Your Conditions:
- PTSD: 70%
- Knee (Left): 30%
- Knee (Right): 30%
- Tinnitus: 10%

Simple Addition: 140% ❌
VA Math Result: 87% → Rounds to 90% ✅

2026 Monthly Payment (with spouse): $2,241.91
```

**Pro Tip:** VA rounds to the nearest 10. At 85%+, you're rated at 90%. At 94%+, you're rated at 100%.

## 📋 Step-by-Step Workflow

### Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1)

1. **Search for primary conditions** using the search bar
2. **Add them to My Packet** (📁 button)
3. **Run Secondary Scout** to find linked conditions
4. **Use MOS Hazard Matcher** to find MOS-specific claims
5. **Check PACT Act Navigator** for toxic exposure presumptives
6. **Review Web of Conditions** to see visual connections

### Phase 2: Evidence Building (Weeks 2-3)

1. **Request your C-File** using FOIA Keysmith (if you don't have it)
2. **Upload C-File** to C-File AI Analyzer when received
3. **Upload Blue Button** records to X-Ray tool
4. **Use Witness Bench** for buddy statements
5. **Use Nexus Builder** for medical connection language
6. **Use Forms Helper** for personal statements
7. **Start Symptom Logger** for frequency-based conditions

### Phase 3: Quality Control (Week 3-4)

1. **Red Team** every statement for weak language
2. **Run Consistency Engine** to find contradictions
3. **Use Statement Analyzer** to remove emotional language
4. **Run Claim Stress Test** to find weaknesses
5. **Fix all identified issues**

### Phase 4: Exam Prep (Week 4)

1. **Use Exam Prep Room** to study DBQ questions
2. **Practice with C&P Exam Simulator**
3. **Print relevant DBQs** to bring to exam
4. **Run The Tribunal** to practice verbal testimony

### Phase 5: Submission

1. **Review everything in My Packet**
2. **Run final Consistency Engine** check
3. **Export backup from The Bunker**
4. **Enable Cloud Sync** for offsite backup
5. **Download all documents** (auto-named by Quartermaster)
6. **Upload to VA.gov** or give to your VSO
7. **Start Time Machine** to track ITF countdown

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: Is this really free?**
A: Yes. 100% free. No ads, no subscriptions, no percentage of backpay.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Your data never leaves your browser unless you explicitly use AI features (with consent) or Cloud Sync to YOUR Google Drive.

**Q: Do I need an account?**
A: No. All data is stored locally in your browser.

**Q: What happens if I clear my browser data?**
A: You'll lose your saved data. Use The Bunker or Cloud Sync to backup regularly.

### AI Features

**Q: Do I need an API key?**
A: AI features require a free Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com.

**Q: Will AI see my personal information?**
A: No. Only condition descriptions are sent. Never SSN, full names, or addresses.

**Q: What if I type something about self-harm?**
A: The Crisis Interceptor detects this LOCALLY and shows Veterans Crisis Line resources immediately. No AI is contacted.

### Claims Process

**Q: Can this replace a VSO?**
A: No. These tools help you prepare, but a VSO provides free accredited representation. Use VSO Finder to locate one.

**Q: Is the data accurate?**
A: Yes. All 751 disabilities are validated against official 38 CFR Parts 3 & 4 (eCFR) as of January 2026.

**Q: Will this guarantee I win my claim?**
A: No tool can guarantee approval. But proper preparation significantly improves your chances.

### Technical

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+

**Q: Can I use this on mobile?**
A: Yes, but desktop provides a better experience for complex tools.

**Q: Can I use this offline?**
A: Partially. Core features work offline after initial load. AI features require internet.
