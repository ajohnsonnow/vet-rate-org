# Quick Start Guide: New Quality of Life Features

## 🏰 The Bunker - Never Lose Your Data Again

### What is it?
Your personal data vault. Backup and restore ALL your claims data with one click.

### When to use it:
- **Before** clearing your browser cache
- **Before** getting a new computer
- **Weekly** as a safety backup
- When you want to work on multiple devices

### How to use:

#### Export (Backup)
1. Click the **🏰 Bunker icon** in the top navigation bar
2. Click **"Download Backup"** button
3. Save the `.json` file somewhere safe (Dropbox, Google Drive, USB drive)

#### Import (Restore)
1. Open The Bunker on any device running Vet-Rate.org
2. Choose **"Replace"** (overwrite all) or **"Merge"** (keep existing, add new)
3. **Drag and drop** your backup file OR click to browse
4. Page will reload with all your data restored!

### Pro Tips:
- Name your backups with dates: `my-vet-rate-backup-2026-01-18.json`
- Keep backups in 3 places: computer, cloud, and USB
- Export before major changes to your claim
- The backup file is plain text JSON - you can inspect it anytime

## ⏰ Time Machine - Never Miss Your ITF Deadline

### What is it?
A countdown timer that shows EXACTLY how many days until your Intent to File expires.

### What's an Intent to File (ITF)?
When you file an ITF with the VA, you have **365 days** to submit your complete claim. Your backpay is calculated from your ITF date-NOT when you finally submit. If your ITF expires, you could lose THOUSANDS in backpay.

### How to use:
1. Click the **⏰ Time Machine icon** in the top navigation
2. Enter the date you filed your Intent to File
3. Select your estimated disability rating (from Tactical Calculator)
4. Click **"Start Countdown"**

### What you'll see:
- **Days remaining** until ITF expires
- **Potential backpay** accumulated so far
- **Backpay at risk** if you miss the deadline
- **Color-coded warnings** as deadline approaches

### Urgency Levels:
- 🟢 **Green** (>60 days): You're good, keep working
- 🟡 **Yellow** (31-60 days): Time to prioritize this claim
- 🔴 **Red** (<30 days): CRITICAL - File ASAP
- ⛔ **Expired**: File new ITF immediately

### Real Example:
```
ITF Date: January 1, 2026
Today: October 1, 2026
Days Remaining: 92 days
Estimated Rating: 70%
Monthly Rate: $1,716

Potential Backpay: $15,444 (9 months accumulated)
At Risk: $5,148 (3 months remaining)

Message: "If you wait 93+ days, you lose $5,148"
```

### Pro Tips:
- Set this up THE DAY you file your ITF
- Check it weekly to stay motivated
- If under 30 days, consider filing a "protective claim" with what you have
- Don't panic if expired-file a new ITF and continue building your packet

## �-️ Commander's Checklist - Track Your Mission Progress

### What is it?
A video-game style progress bar that shows you EXACTLY how ready you are to file your claim.

### Where is it?
Always visible at the **bottom of the screen**. Click it to expand and see details.

### What it tracks:
9 key milestones, each worth points based on importance:

1. ✅ **Veteran Profile** (5 pts) - Basic info entered
2. ✅ **Diagnosis Found** (15 pts) - Medical evidence obtained
3. ✅ **Service Connection** (15 pts) - Linked to military service
4. ✅ **Symptoms Logged** (10 pts) - Daily symptom tracking
5. ✅ **Statement Written** (15 pts) - Personal statement drafted
6. ✅ **Nexus Letter** (15 pts) - Medical reasoning documented
7. ✅ **Secondaries Found** (10 pts) - Connected conditions identified
8. ✅ **Ratings Calculated** (10 pts) - Combined rating estimated
9. ✅ **Forms Prepared** (5 pts) - VA forms filled out

**Total: 100 points = 100% ready**

### How it helps:
- **Eliminates overwhelm**: Shows exactly what's done and what's left
- **Provides direction**: Click incomplete items to jump to the right tool
- **Motivates you**: Small wins add up to completion
- **Prevents missing steps**: Ensures you don't skip critical evidence

### Progress Messages:
- 0-25%: "🎯 Let's get started on your mission!"
- 25-50%: "🚀 Good start! Let's build momentum!"
- 50-80%: "💪 You're halfway there! Keep going!"
- 80-100%: "🔥 Almost there! Just a few more steps!"
- 100%: "🎉 Mission Complete! You're ready to file!"

### Pro Tips:
- You don't need 100% to file-but more evidence = stronger claim
- Focus on high-value items first (diagnosis, nexus, statement = 45 points)
- Check the checklist before filing to catch anything you missed
- Use it as your daily to-do list

## Usage Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Getting Started
1. Open Vet-Rate.org for the first time
2. See Commander's Checklist at 0%: "🎯 Let's get started!"
3. Set up Time Machine with your ITF date (if you have one)
4. Export first backup to The Bunker (even if empty-good habit)
5. Start working through checklist milestones

### Scenario 2: Switching Computers
1. On old computer: Open The Bunker → Download Backup
2. Save file to USB or cloud storage
3. On new computer: Open Vet-Rate.org
4. Open The Bunker → Import your backup file
5. Everything restored! Continue where you left off

### Scenario 3: 30 Days Before ITF Expires
1. Time Machine turns RED and shows: "⚠️ CRITICAL: 30 days left"
2. Check Commander's Checklist: "You're 75% complete"
3. See 2 incomplete items: Nexus Letter and Forms
4. Click incomplete items to jump directly to those tools
5. Complete them, file claim with 2 weeks to spare

### Scenario 4: Weekly Routine
Every Sunday:
1. Export backup to The Bunker (takes 10 seconds)
2. Check Time Machine: "How much backpay have I accumulated?"
3. Review Commander's Checklist: "What can I complete this week?"
4. Pick one incomplete milestone and work on it

## FAQ

### Q: Can I use this on mobile?
**A:** Yes! All features are fully responsive. The Bunker works great for backing up your phone's data.

### Q: Is my data private?
**A:** YES. Everything stays on YOUR device. The Bunker creates local files-nothing is sent to any server.

### Q: What if I have multiple claims?
**A:** Time Machine currently tracks one ITF at a time. Set it for your oldest/most important claim.

### Q: Can I share my backup with a VSO?
**A:** Yes! The backup file is plain JSON. However, it contains ALL your personal info, so share securely.

### Q: What if I forget to export a backup?
**A:** Commander's Checklist and Time Machine data persist in your browser. But export regularly just in case!

### Q: Do I need all three features?
**A:** 
- **The Bunker**: CRITICAL - Use this weekly
- **Time Machine**: CRITICAL if you have an ITF
- **Commander's Checklist**: HELPFUL - Keeps you on track

### Q: Can I turn off Commander's Checklist?
**A:** Currently it's always visible (it's that important). Future update may add a minimize option.
