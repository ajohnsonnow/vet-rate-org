## Color Scheme Reference

### The Bunker
- Primary: Blue (#2563eb)
- Accent: Blue/White gradient
- Success: Green (#16a34a)
- Border: Blue (#60a5fa)

### Time Machine
- Safe (>60d): Blue (#2563eb)
- Urgent (31-60d): Yellow (#eab308) → Orange (#f97316)
- Critical (<30d): Red (#dc2626) + Pulse animation
- Expired: Dark Red (#991b1b)

### Commander's Checklist
- 0-25%: Gray (#6b7280)
- 25-50%: Orange (#f97316)
- 50-80%: Yellow (#eab308)
- 80-99%: Blue (#2563eb)
- 100%: Green (#16a34a) + Celebration
