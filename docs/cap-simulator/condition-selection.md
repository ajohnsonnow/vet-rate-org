# C&P Simulator - Condition Selection

Choose a condition to practice for your C&P exam preparation.

---

## Selection Interface

After choosing Simulation Mode, you'll see the condition selection screen with:

1. **Your Saved Conditions** - From My Packet
2. **Search All Conditions** - Full database search
3. **Browse by Category** - Body system categories

---

## From My Packet

If you have conditions saved in My Packet, they appear at the top:

### Your Conditions Section

```
📁 Your Saved Conditions (3)
├── PTSD (9411) - Primary
├── Sleep Apnea (6847) - Secondary to PTSD
└── Lumbar Strain (5237) - Primary
```

### Why Use Saved Conditions?

- ✅ Pre-selected from your actual claims
- ✅ Quick access to relevant conditions
- ✅ Tracks with your overall claim strategy

## Condition Card Preview

When you hover over or select a condition, you see:

### Condition Information

- **Name** - Official VA condition name
- **Diagnostic Code** - 4-digit identifier
- **Body System** - Category
- **Available Ratings** - Possible percentages

### Simulation Preview

- **Question Count** - How many questions to expect
- **Estimated Time** - How long the simulation takes
- **Difficulty** - Based on rating complexity

---

## Supported Conditions

The simulator has specialized questions for:

### Fully Supported (Detailed Questions)

- Mental Health conditions (4.130 formula)
- Musculoskeletal/ROM conditions (4.71a)
- Respiratory conditions (4.97)
- Cardiovascular conditions (4.104)
- Spine conditions with IVDS

### Generic Support (Standard Questions)

- Other body systems
- Less common conditions
- Analogous-rated conditions

!!! info "Generic Questions"
    Conditions without specialized question sets still receive relevant questions about severity, frequency, and functional impact.

## Tips for Selection

!!! tip "Choosing Wisely"
    
    - **Start with your most complex condition** - Usually your primary
    - **Practice conditions before actual exams** - Use realistic timing
    - **Try conditions you're unsure about** - Learn the criteria
    - **Practice multiple times** - Different approaches may yield insights
