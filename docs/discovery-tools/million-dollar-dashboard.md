# Million Dollar Dashboard

Million Dollar Dashboard - "The Whoa Factor" - projects the **true lifetime value** of your VA disability rating. Most veterans think in terms of "$X a month." This tool shows what that monthly payment, plus the benefits that come with it, adds up to over a lifetime.

## Launching Million Dollar Dashboard

On the home page, scroll to the **💎 Shock & Awe Tools** section and click **"💵 Show Me The Money"**. It's also reachable from the header's **🛠️ Tools → Calculate** menu.

![Million Dollar Dashboard showing a projected lifetime value](../assets/images/screenshots/million-dollar-dashboard/million-dollar-dashboard-default.png)
_Million Dollar Dashboard combines VA compensation, property tax savings, education benefits, and healthcare value into one lifetime projection._

---

## Your Profile Inputs

The projection is driven by a few numbers you control:

- **Current age**
- **VA Rating %** - type it directly, or click **Load** to pull in your saved ratings and let the calculator determine your combined percentage
- **State** - used to look up property tax exemption eligibility
- **Spouse?** and **# Children** - affects both VA compensation rate and Chapter 35 education benefit eligibility
- **P&T?** - whether the rating is Permanent & Total, which unlocks Chapter 35 Dependents Education Assistance for eligible dependents

## What's Included in the Total

| Category                   | How It's Calculated                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **VA Compensation**        | Monthly pay at your rating and dependent status, projected forward with an estimated annual Cost of Living Adjustment (COLA)                     |
| **Property Tax Exemption** | State-specific exemption value, only counted if your rating meets that state's actual minimum threshold (not assumed to be 100% for every state) |
| **Chapter 35 DEA**         | Education benefit value for eligible dependents, available once your rating is 100%                                                              |
| **CHAMPVA Healthcare**     | Estimated annual family healthcare premium savings                                                                                               |

The dashboard also charts **cumulative value growth** year by year, so you can see how the total compounds over time rather than just the final number.

---

## Why the Number Looks So Large

A 100% Permanent & Total rating isn't just "a few thousand a month" - projected over a normal lifespan with COLA increases, property tax savings, and dependent benefits, the combined value routinely reaches **seven figures**. This dashboard exists to make that concrete, especially for veterans who are on the fence about pursuing a rating increase or additional secondary claims.

---

## Important Disclaimer

!!! warning "Projection, Not a Promise"
Million Dollar Dashboard's totals are **estimates based on current-year rates, your inputs, and assumptions about COLA and life expectancy** - actual future VA compensation rates, state benefit rules, and your own life circumstances will vary. This tool does not guarantee any particular VA rating, benefit eligibility, or dollar amount.

    For personalized guidance, seek help from an accredited **Veterans Service Officer (VSO)** - free, no fees allowed - and/or a VA-accredited attorney or claims agent. Find one at [va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation](https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/).
