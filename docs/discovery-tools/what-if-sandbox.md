# What-If Sandbox

The What-If Sandbox is a **visual, drag-and-drop scenario planner** for testing combined-rating math. Add hypothetical conditions and ratings, and watch your combined rating and estimated monthly pay recalculate in real time - without saving anything to a real claim.

## Why Use a Sandbox Instead of the Real Calculator?

Tactical Calculator is for **tracking your actual claim conditions**. What-If Sandbox is for **exploring possibilities** - "What if I also filed for sleep apnea?" or "What would my rating be if this bilateral condition came in at 30% instead of 20%?" - without touching your saved claims or ratings.

## The Math Behind It

What-If Sandbox uses the same combined-ratings engine as Tactical Calculator:

- **38 CFR § 4.25** - the VA's combined ratings table (ratings don't simply add)
- **38 CFR § 4.26** - the Bilateral Factor, automatically applied when you add matching left/right conditions (e.g. Left Knee + Right Knee)
- Current-year VA compensation rates for the resulting combined percentage

## Loading Your Real Data

What-If Sandbox can pull in your currently saved claims or ratings as a starting point, so you can model "what if I add one more condition to what I already have" instead of building a scenario from scratch.

## Important Disclaimer

!!! warning "Educational Tool"
What-If Sandbox scenarios are **hypothetical** and for planning purposes only. Nothing here is saved to a real claim, and the results do not guarantee any particular VA rating or claim outcome.
