## Quick Summary

### Not Legal Advice

Nothing on Vet-Rate.org constitutes legal advice. For legal matters:

- Consult a VA-accredited attorney
- Contact a VSO representative
- Seek qualified legal counsel

### Not Medical Advice

Nothing on Vet-Rate.org constitutes medical advice. For medical matters:

- Consult your healthcare provider
- Seek qualified medical opinions
- Contact VA healthcare

### Not VA Affiliated

Vet-Rate.org is:

- **Independent** - Not affiliated with the VA
- **Unofficial** - Not an official VA resource
- **Educational** - For informational purposes only

### No Guarantees

Using Vet-Rate.org does not guarantee:

- Any specific claim outcome
- VA approval of claims
- Accuracy of estimated ratings
- Success with the VA
