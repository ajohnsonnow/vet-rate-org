# Privacy Policy

How Vet-Rate.org handles your privacy.

*Last Updated: June 2026*

---

## Overview

Vet-Rate.org is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our Service.

**The short version:** We don't collect your personal data — everything you enter stays on your device. The only analytics is GoatCounter: cookieless, aggregate page views with no personal identifiers.

---

## Information We DO NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

- ❌ Personal identification information
- ❌ Email addresses
- ❌ Names
- ❌ IP addresses (stored)
- ❌ Personally-identifying usage analytics
- ❌ Cross-site or cross-session tracking
- ❌ Cookies for tracking
- ❌ Any data you enter into the application

We do use **GoatCounter** for minimal, cookieless, aggregate analytics — see
[Third Parties](#third-parties) below.

---

## How the Service Works

### Local Processing

All functionality happens in your web browser:

- Searches run locally
- Calculations happen on your device
- Nothing is sent to remote servers

### Local Storage

Data you save is stored in your browser's localStorage:

- Only accessible on your device
- Only in the browser you used
- Controlled entirely by you

---

## Data You Enter

### What Happens to Your Data

When you enter information (conditions, statements, forms):

1. Data is processed in your browser
2. Data is saved to browser localStorage
3. Data never leaves your device
4. Data is never transmitted to any server

### Your Control

You can:

- View your data (browser developer tools)
- Export your data (backup feature)
- Delete your data (clear site data)

---

## Third Parties

### Analytics (GoatCounter)

We use **GoatCounter** — a privacy-respecting, open-source, cookieless analytics tool — to understand basic, aggregate site usage (page views, referrer, country-level location). It sets no cookies, stores no personal identifiers, and does not track you across sites or sessions.

We do **not** use:

- Google Analytics
- Facebook Pixel
- Advertising networks
- Any analytics tool that profiles or identifies individual users

### No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not share data with third parties because we don't have your data to share.

### External Links

The Service contains links to external sites (VA.gov, etc.). When you click these links:

- You leave Vet-Rate.org
- Those sites have their own privacy policies
- We recommend reviewing their policies

---

## Hosting

### Static File Hosting

Vet-Rate.org is hosted as static files. The hosting provider:

- Serves the application files
- Does not receive your personal data
- May log standard server access (IP addresses accessing the site)

We do not control or have access to hosting provider logs.

---

## Cookies

### Cookies We Use

We may use strictly necessary cookies for:

- Session management
- Preference storage (theme, accessibility settings)

### Cookies We Don't Use

We do not use cookies for:

- Tracking
- Analytics
- Advertising
- Third-party purposes

## Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Security

### Our Measures

- HTTPS encryption for all connections
- No server-side data storage
- Regular security reviews

### Your Responsibilities

Since data is stored locally:

- Keep your device secure
- Use trusted networks
- Back up your data regularly

---

## Data Retention

### We Retain Nothing

We have no data to retain. Your data exists only on your device.

### Your Local Data

Data in your browser localStorage persists until:

- You delete it
- You clear browser data
- Browser storage is cleared

You can access all your data through browser developer tools.

### Portability

You can export all your data using the backup feature.

### Deletion

You can delete all data by clearing site data in your browser.

### No Account

There is no account to delete or manage.
