# What is a Nexus Statement?

Understanding the purpose, components, and importance of a nexus statement in your VA claim.

---

## Definition

A **nexus statement** is a document that establishes the connection between your current medical condition and your military service. It articulates:

- **What** — The condition you have
- **When** — When it started or worsened
- **Why** — How it connects to military service
- **How** — The medical theory supporting the connection

---

## Types of Nexus Evidence

### Medical Nexus Opinion (IMO/INO)

A **medical nexus letter** from a qualified healthcare provider:

- Written by MD, DO, NP, PA, or specialist
- Provides medical opinion on connection
- Uses "at least as likely as not" standard
- Most persuasive form of nexus evidence

### Statement in Support of Claim

A **personal statement** you write:

- Describes your experience in your own words
- Provides context and details
- Supplements medical evidence
- Explains timeline and events

    The Nexus Builder helps you create a **Statement in Support of Claim** — your personal account that supplements medical evidence.

---

## Legal Standard

For the VA to grant service connection, the evidence must show:

> "At least as likely as not" (50% or greater probability)

This means the connection between your condition and service is:

- Equally or more likely than not
- Not "more likely than not" (51%+)
- Not "possible" or "conceivable"

---

## Elements of a Strong Nexus

### 1. Current Diagnosis

You must have a **current diagnosed condition**:

- Medical records showing diagnosis
- Recent treatment for the condition
- C&P exam confirming diagnosis

### 2. In-Service Event

For **direct connection**, evidence of:

- Event during military service
- Injury documented in service records
- Exposure to hazards
- Onset of symptoms in service

### 3. Medical Connection

The medical link between the two:

- How the event led to the condition
- Medical literature supporting the theory
- Scientific basis for the connection

---

## Statement in Support of Claim

### Purpose

Your personal statement serves to:

- Provide your first-person account
- Fill gaps in medical records
- Explain circumstances
- Describe impact on your life

### What to Include

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Identity** | Your name, claim number, dates |
| **Condition** | What you're claiming |
| **Service Details** | When, where, what happened |
| **Timeline** | Onset, progression, treatment |
| **Symptoms** | What you experience |
| **Impact** | How it affects daily life |
| **Treatment** | Medical care received |

### What NOT to Include

- Medical opinions (unless you're qualified)
- Exaggerations or speculation
- Unrelated complaints
- Complaints about the VA process

---

## How Statements Support Claims

### Filling Documentation Gaps

Military medical records may be:

- Incomplete
- Missing
- Destroyed (especially for older records)

Your statement provides context where records are lacking.

### Explaining Circumstances

Records show what happened, but not always:

- Why you didn't seek treatment
- The full impact of injuries
- Ongoing symptoms between visits

### Connecting the Dots

Medical records are snapshots. Your statement shows:

- The continuous thread of symptoms
- How in-service events led to current issues
- The progression over time

## Example Statement Structure

```
STATEMENT IN SUPPORT OF CLAIM

I, [NAME], hereby state the following in support of my 
claim for service connection for [CONDITION]:

MILITARY SERVICE
I served in the United States [Branch] from [Start Date] 
to [End Date]. My MOS was [MOS/Rating], and I was stationed 
at [Locations].

IN-SERVICE EVENT
During my service, [describe the event, injury, or exposure]. 
This occurred on or about [date] at [location].

ONSET OF CONDITION
Following this event, I began experiencing [symptoms]. 
These symptoms have [continued/worsened] since that time.

CURRENT SYMPTOMS
Currently, I experience [specific symptoms]. These symptoms 
occur [frequency] and last [duration]. The severity ranges 
from [mild description] to [severe description].

IMPACT ON DAILY LIFE
This condition affects my daily life in the following ways:
- [Work impact]
- [Relationship impact]  
- [Daily activity impact]

MEDICAL TREATMENT
I have sought medical treatment for this condition from:
- [Provider/Facility] from [dates]
- [Current treatment plan]

CONCLUSION
Based on the foregoing, I respectfully request that service 
connection be granted for [condition].

I certify that the statements made herein are true and 
correct to the best of my knowledge and belief.

_____________________________
[Signature]
[Date]
```

---

## Tips for Effective Statements

!!! tip "Writing Your Statement"
    
    1. **Be specific with dates** — Approximate if necessary ("On or about...")
    2. **Use first-person** — "I experienced..." not "The veteran experienced..."
    3. **Stay factual** — Describe what happened, not what you think happened
    4. **Include impacts** — How does it affect your work, relationships, activities?
    5. **Keep it focused** — One condition per statement is clearest
    6. **Sign and date** — Don't forget!
