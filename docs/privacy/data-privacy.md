# Data Privacy

Detailed information about how your data is handled in Vet-Rate.org.

---

## Core Privacy Principles

### 1. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

### 2. Local Processing Only

All functionality happens in your browser on your device.

### 3. No Tracking

No analytics, cookies for tracking, or behavioral monitoring.

### 4. No Accounts

No registration, logins, or identity verification.

### 5. You Own Your Data

You control everything - export, delete, or keep as you wish.

---

## What You Enter

### Personal Information

When you enter information like:

- Your name
- Service dates
- Medical conditions
- Symptoms
- Personal statements

This information:

- ✅ Stays in your browser
- ✅ Is stored in localStorage
- ✅ Never transmitted anywhere
- ✅ Only you can access it
- ❌ Is not sent to any server
- ❌ Is not shared with anyone
- ❌ Is not used for any analytics

### Search Queries

When you search for conditions:

- ✅ Search happens in your browser
- ✅ Against a static database loaded locally
- ❌ Queries are not logged
- ❌ Queries are not transmitted

---

## Data Storage Details

### Where Data is Stored

| Data | Location | Accessible By |
|------|----------|---------------|
| My Packet | Browser localStorage | Only you |
| Settings | Browser localStorage | Only you |
| Forms | Browser localStorage | Only you |
| Profile | Browser localStorage | Only you |

### How Long Data is Stored

Data persists until:

- You delete it manually
- You clear browser data
- You clear site-specific data
- Browser storage is cleared

### Who Can Access

Only you can access your data:

- No backend servers with copies
- No database storing your information
- No administrators with access

## Third Parties

### What Third Parties Receive

| Third Party | What They Receive |
|-------------|-------------------|
| **Hosting Provider** | None of your data |

### External Links

When you click external links (like VA.gov):

- You leave Vet-Rate.org
- Those sites have their own privacy policies
- We recommend reviewing their policies

## Browser Security

### Protecting Your Data

Since data is stored in your browser:

1. **Use a personal device** - Avoid shared computers
2. **Use a secure browser** - Keep it updated
3. **Don't use public computers** - Data could remain
4. **Consider private browsing** - For extra privacy
5. **Backup your data** - Protect against loss

## Your Rights

### Right to Access

You can view all your data:

1. Open browser developer tools (F12)
2. Go to Application → Local Storage
3. View Vet-Rate.org data

### Right to Export

You can export all your data:

1. Open My Packet
2. Click Backup/Export
3. Download the file

### Right to Delete

You can delete all your data:

1. Clear site data in browser settings
2. Or use in-app clear function

### Right to Not Provide

You're not required to enter any personal information. The tool works without it.

## Special Categories of Data

### Health Information

Vet-Rate.org may contain health information you enter:

- Medical conditions
- Symptoms
- Treatment history

This information:

- Is protected by local-only storage
- Is never transmitted
- Is controlled entirely by you
- Should be protected on your device

### Military Information

Service-related information you enter:

- Dates of service
- Duty stations
- In-service events

Same protections apply - never leaves your device.

## Changes to Data Handling

If we ever change how data is handled:

- We will update the Privacy Policy
- Changes will be announced
- Existing data handling won't change retroactively
- You will always have control
