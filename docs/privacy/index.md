## Our Privacy Commitment

Vet-Rate.org is built on a fundamental principle: **your data stays with you**.

We believe veterans' personal information deserves the highest level of protection, which is why we designed a system that processes everything locally on your device.

## Privacy at a Glance

### What We DON'T Collect

| Data Type | Collected? |
|-----------|------------|
| Personal Information | ❌ No |
| Email Addresses | ❌ No |
| IP Addresses | ❌ No |
| Usage Analytics | ❌ No |
| Your Saved Conditions | ❌ No |
| Your Statements | ❌ No |
| Your Forms | ❌ No |
| Anything You Enter | ❌ No |

### What We DO

| Feature | How |
|---------|-----|
| **Process Locally** | Everything runs in your browser |
| **Store Locally** | Data saved in your browser storage |
| **No Accounts** | No registration required |
| **No Tracking** | No analytics or tracking scripts |
| **Open Information** | Disability data from public sources |

## Your Control

You have complete control over your data:

### View Your Data

All data is in your browser — you can inspect it anytime using browser developer tools.

### Export Your Data

Backup all your data to a file you control.

### Delete Your Data

Remove all stored data at any time.

### No Account to Manage

No account means no account security to worry about.
