# Rating Tools

The **Rating Tools** cluster helps you protect the rating you already have, build the evidence that justifies a higher one, and understand VA payment mechanics like effective dates and retroactive pay. These nine tools cover defense (don't accidentally lower your rating), evidence-building (logs, timelines, body maps), and money (PACT Act presumptions, FOIA requests, retro pay).

## How These Tools Fit Together

<div class="step-container">
<div class="step">
<strong>Protect what you have</strong> - Run Risk Assessment before filing anything new, especially if you're already at a high combined rating
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Build ongoing evidence</strong> - Use Symptom Logger and Evidence Timeline to document frequency and continuity over time, not just a single doctor visit
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Find what you're entitled to</strong> - Check PACT Act Navigator for presumptive conditions and Retro Pay Hunter for missed back pay
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>See what the VA sees</strong> - Use the FOIA Generator to request your C-File so you know what evidence is already in your record
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Pressure-test before you file</strong> - Run your statement through Claim Stress Test and practice with The Tribunal before a real hearing
</div>
</div>

## Important Disclaimer

!!! warning "No Guarantee of Outcome"
These tools are **educational and organizational aids** — none of them guarantee any particular VA rating or claim outcome. Ratings and decisions are made solely by the VA.

    Before filing, appealing, or making a strategic decision based on any tool in this section, have a **VSO (Veterans Service Officer)** — free, no fees allowed — or a **VA-accredited attorney or claims agent** review your case. Find one at [va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation](https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/).
