# CFR Reference

Guide to relevant Code of Federal Regulations sections for VA disability claims.

## Title 38 - Veterans' Benefits

Title 38 of the Code of Federal Regulations contains all VA-related regulations.

### Access the eCFR

| **§ 3.307** | Presumptive service connection |
| **§ 3.309** | Presumptive diseases |
| **§ 3.310** | Secondary service connection |
| **§ 3.317** | Gulf War presumptives |
| **§ 3.318** | Agent Orange presumptives |

### § 3.310 - Secondary Service Connection

> "Disability which is proximately due to or the result of a service-connected disease or injury shall be service connected."

Key points:

- Secondary conditions must be caused OR aggravated
- Medical nexus required
- Must have existing service-connected condition

## Part 4 - Schedule for Rating Disabilities

The rating schedule containing criteria for all rated conditions.

### General Provisions

| Section | Topic |
|---------|-------|
| **§ 4.1** | Essentials of evaluative rating |
| **§ 4.2** | Interpretation of examination reports |
| **§ 4.3** | Reasonable doubt |
| **§ 4.6** | Evaluation of evidence |
| **§ 4.7** | Higher rating when symptoms between levels |
| **§ 4.10** | Functional impairment |

### § 4.3 - Reasonable Doubt

> "When there is an approximate balance of positive and negative evidence... the benefit of the doubt will be given to the claimant."

### § 4.7 - Higher of Two Evaluations

> "Where there is a question as to which of two evaluations shall be applied, the higher evaluation will be assigned if the disability picture more nearly approximates the criteria required for that rating."

## Using CFR in Claims

### In Your Statements

Reference relevant sections:

> "Per 38 CFR § 3.310, my [secondary condition] was caused by my service-connected [primary condition]."

### For Understanding Criteria

Read the specific criteria for your condition to understand:

- What the VA evaluates
- What separates rating levels
- How to document your symptoms

### With Your VSO/Attorney

Knowing the regulations helps you:

- Ask informed questions
- Understand decisions
- Prepare appeals
