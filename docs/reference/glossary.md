# Glossary

Common terms used in VA disability claims.

---

## A

### Aggravation
When military service permanently worsens a pre-existing condition beyond its natural progression.

### Appeal
A formal request to review a VA decision you disagree with.

### At Least As Likely As Not
The evidentiary standard for VA claims (50% or greater probability).

---

## B

### Bilateral Factor
Additional compensation (10%) when disabilities affect both limbs (arms or legs).

### Board of Veterans Appeals (BVA)
Higher-level review board for VA claim appeals.

### Buddy Statement
Written statement from someone who can attest to your condition or service events.

---

## C

### C&P Exam (Compensation and Pension)
Medical examination by VA to evaluate your claimed disability.

### CFR (Code of Federal Regulations)
Federal regulations including disability rating criteria (Title 38).

### Claim
A formal request for VA disability benefits.

### Combined Rating
Your total disability rating calculated using VA math.

### Concurrent Receipt
Receiving both military retirement pay and VA disability compensation.

---

## D

### DBQ (Disability Benefits Questionnaire)
Standardized form used by examiners to evaluate disabilities.

### Decision Letter
Official VA notification of claim decision.

### DeLuca Factors
Additional functional loss factors: pain, weakness, fatigability, incoordination.

### Dependency and Indemnity Compensation (DIC)
Benefits paid to surviving dependents.

### Diagnostic Code
4-digit code identifying specific disabilities in the rating schedule.

### Direct Service Connection
Connection between current condition and specific in-service event.

---

## E

### eBenefits
VA online portal for managing benefits.

### Effective Date
The date from which benefits are paid (often claim date or Intent to File date).

### Evidence
Documentation supporting your claim (medical records, statements, etc.).

---

## F

### Favorable Finding
When VA agrees with an element of your claim.

### Fully Developed Claim (FDC)
Claim submitted with all available evidence, allowing faster processing.

### Functional Impairment
How your condition affects your ability to function.

---

## G

### General Rating Formula
Standard criteria used to rate certain body systems (e.g., mental health).

---

## H

### Higher-Level Review
Appeal option where a senior reviewer examines your claim.

---

## I

### Independent Medical Opinion (IMO)
Medical opinion from a private physician (often for nexus letters).

### Intent to File (ITF)
Notification to VA of intent to file a claim, protecting effective date.

### IVDS (Intervertebral Disc Syndrome)
Spine condition with specific rating criteria.

---

## L

### Lay Evidence
Non-medical evidence, such as your own statements or buddy statements.

---

## M

### Medical Evidence
Documentation from medical providers.

### METs (Metabolic Equivalents)
Measure of exercise capacity used in cardiac ratings.

### Military Sexual Trauma (MST)
Sexual assault or harassment during military service.

---

## N

### Nexus
The medical connection between your condition and military service.

### Nexus Letter
Medical opinion establishing connection between condition and service.

### Notice of Disagreement (NOD)
Formal document initiating an appeal.

---

## P

### PACT Act
Legislation expanding presumptive conditions for toxic exposures.

### Presumptive Condition
Condition assumed to be service-connected based on service type/location.

### Primary Condition
Condition directly connected to service (not secondary to another condition).

---

## R

### Range of Motion (ROM)
Measurement of joint movement in degrees.

### Rating Decision
VA's determination of your disability percentage.

### Rating Schedule
38 CFR Part 4 - criteria for evaluating disabilities.

---

## S

### Secondary Condition
Condition caused or aggravated by a service-connected condition.

### Service Connection
VA's recognition that a condition is related to military service.

### SMC (Special Monthly Compensation)
Additional compensation for severe disabilities or combinations.

### Statement in Support of Claim
Your written account supporting your claim (VA Form 21-4138).

### Supplemental Claim
New claim with new evidence after a denial.

---

## T

### TDIU (Total Disability Individual Unemployability)
Compensation at 100% rate when disabilities prevent employment.

---

## V

### VA (Department of Veterans Affairs)
Federal agency administering veteran benefits.

### VA Math
Method for calculating combined disability ratings.

### VSO (Veterans Service Organization)
Organizations providing free claims assistance (DAV, VFW, American Legion, etc.).

---

## W

### WALA (World Presumptive Locations)
Areas where veterans may have presumptive conditions.
