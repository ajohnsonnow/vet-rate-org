## Quick Reference Cards

### Rating Math

Combined ratings use VA math, not simple addition:

```
First Rating:  50%
Remaining:     50% healthy

Second Rating: 30% of remaining
30% × 50% =    15%

Combined:      50% + 15% = 65%
Rounded:       70% (rounds to nearest 10)
```

### Service Connection Requirements

To establish service connection:

1. **Current Diagnosis** - You have the condition now
2. **In-Service Event** - Something happened in service
3. **Nexus** - Medical link between them

### Evidence Standard

**"At least as likely as not"**

- Means 50% or greater probability
- Not "more likely than not"
- Not "possible" or "conceivable"
