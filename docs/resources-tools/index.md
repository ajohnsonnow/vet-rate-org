# Resources & Tools

Beyond the claim-building tools, Vet-Rate.org includes a set of **support, transparency, and data-control tools** that don't fit neatly into research, evidence, or rating categories. This section covers finding free professional help, tracking regulatory changes, understanding how AI is used (both by the VA and by this app), and controlling your own local data.

| VA AI Transparency Hub       | Home page                                                                                 |

| VKB Viewer                   | Header Tools menu ("📚 Knowledge Base")                                                   |
| Mission Protocol             | **Footer only** ("🎖️ Our Promise")                                                        |
| Publications Library         | **Footer only** ("📚 Pubs Library")                                                       |

## Important Disclaimer

!!! warning "Educational Resources Only"
None of the tools in this section are legal, medical, or official VA advice. VSO Finder and Ask the Regs specifically point you toward **free, accredited** help - a VSO or accredited attorney - rather than replacing it. Always verify any AI-generated answer against the official 38 CFR text or a qualified representative before relying on it.
