# Adding to My Packet

Learn how to save promising secondary conditions from Secondary Scout to your claims packet for tracking and evidence building.

---

## Why Save to My Packet?

Saving secondary conditions to My Packet helps you:

- **Track potential claims** — Organize your secondary claim ideas
- **Build evidence** — Generate nexus statements for each
- **Prepare systematically** — Work through conditions one at a time
- **Backup your work** — Export and restore your packet

---

## Individual Save

### Saving a Single Condition

<div class="step-container">
<div class="step">
<strong>Find a promising suggestion</strong> — Review the Secondary Scout results
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Expand the card</strong> — Click to see details
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Click "Save to Packet"</strong> — Button in the card actions
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Confirmation</strong> — Button changes to "Saved ✓"
</div>
</div>

### What Gets Saved

When you save a secondary condition, the following information is stored:

| Data | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Condition Name** | The secondary condition |
| **Parent Condition** | The primary it's linked to |
| **Probability** | High or Medium |
| **Mechanism** | Type of connection |
| **Nexus Theory** | Medical explanation |
| **Medical Citations** | Supporting references |
| **Status** | "Drafting" (default) |

## Bulk Save

### Saving Multiple Conditions at Once

<div class="step-container">
<div class="step">
<strong>Select conditions</strong> — Check the boxes on desired cards
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Or click "Select All"</strong> — Selects all unsaved suggestions
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Review selection</strong> — Floating bar shows count
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Click "Add to Packet"</strong> — On the floating action bar
</div>
<div class="step">
<strong>Confirmation</strong> — Alert shows how many were added
</div>
</div>

### Floating Action Bar

When conditions are selected:

```
╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  📋 X claims selected                    [Add to Packet] [✕]  ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

- Shows count of selected items
- **Add to Packet** — Saves all selected
- **X** — Clears selection

---

## Already Saved Indicator

For conditions already in your packet:

- **"Save to Packet"** button is disabled
- Shows **"Saved ✓"** instead
- Checkbox is disabled in bulk selection
- Card may show "Already saved" indicator

---

## Accessing My Packet

After saving, access your conditions:

### From Secondary Scout

Click **"My Packet"** button in the results header.

### From Header Navigation

Click **"📁 My Packet"** in the main navigation.

### Shortcut Button

Some cards show **"View in Packet"** after saving.

---

## What to Do Next

After saving secondary conditions to your packet:

### 1. Build Nexus Statements

For each saved condition:

1. Open My Packet
2. Find the condition
3. Click **"Build Statement"**
4. Complete the Nexus Builder wizard
5. Download your statement

### 2. Update Status

Track your progress:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Drafting** | Initial research stage |
| **Statement Generated** | Nexus statement created |
| **Filed** | Claim submitted to VA |

### 3. Gather Evidence

For each secondary condition:

- Medical records showing diagnosis
- Treatment records
- Nexus letter from physician
- Buddy statements
- Symptom journal/log

---

## Removing from Packet

If you decide not to pursue a secondary condition:

1. Open **My Packet**
2. Find the condition
3. Click **"Remove"** button
4. Confirm deletion

!!! warning "Permanent Action"
    Removing a claim also deletes any associated statements. Consider exporting your packet backup first.

---

## Best Practices

!!! tip "Recommended Workflow"
    
    1. **Start with High probability** — Focus on strongest connections
    2. **Research each one** — Read the nexus theory and citations
    3. **Prioritize** — You can't file everything at once
    4. **Build evidence** — Use Nexus Builder for each
    5. **Consult professionals** — Talk to VSO before filing
    6. **File strategically** — Consider filing in batches

---

## Data Storage

Secondary conditions saved to My Packet are:

- ✅ Stored locally in your browser
- ✅ Never sent to any server
- ✅ Persistent until you clear browser data
- ✅ Exportable via backup feature
- ❌ Not synced across devices
- ❌ Not accessible on other browsers

!!! tip "Regular Backups"
    Use My Packet's backup feature regularly to export your saved claims. Store the backup file securely on your device or cloud storage.
