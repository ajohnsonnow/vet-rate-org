| Organization | Focus | Website |
|--------------|-------|---------|

| **DAV** | Disabled veterans | dav.org |
| **AMVETS** | All veterans | amvets.org |
| **PVA** | Paralyzed veterans | pva.org |
| **VVA** | Vietnam veterans | vva.org |
| **IAVA** | Post-9/11 veterans | iava.org |
| **WWP** | Wounded warriors | woundedwarriorproject.org |

### What VSOs Provide

- **Claims Assistance** — Free help filing claims
- **Representation** — Advocates with VA
- **Appeals Help** — Assistance with denied claims
- **Benefits Counseling** — Explain your options
- **Local Support** — Community chapters

### Finding a VSO Representative

1. Visit VA's accredited representative search:
   **va.gov/vso**
2. Contact VSO directly
3. Visit your County Veterans Service Office

## Legal Resources

### VA-Accredited Attorneys

For complex claims or appeals:

- **NOVA** — National Organization of Veterans' Advocates
  nova.org
- **NVLSP** — National Veterans Legal Services Program
  nvlsp.org

### Pro Bono Legal Help

Free legal assistance:

- **Veterans Consortium Pro Bono Program**
  vetsprobono.org
- **State Bar Veteran Programs**
  Check your state bar association

### When to Get Legal Help

Consider an attorney for:

- Complex appeals
- Claims Court cases
- CUE (Clear and Unmistakable Error) claims
- Long-denied claims

!!! info "VSO First"
    For most initial claims and simple appeals, a VSO is sufficient and free. Attorneys typically charge fees (usually contingent on winning).

## State & Local Resources

### State Veterans Affairs Offices

Every state has a Department of Veterans Affairs:

- Additional state benefits
- Property tax exemptions
- Hunting/fishing licenses
- State veteran homes
- Education benefits

**Find yours:** Search "[Your State] Department of Veterans Affairs"

### County Veterans Service Officers (CVSOs)

Free local assistance:

- Claims help
- Benefits counseling
- Local resources
- Transportation
- Emergency assistance

**Find yours:** Search "[Your County] Veterans Service Officer"

## Community Forums

### Online Communities

!!! warning "Verify Information"
    While peer communities are helpful, always verify advice with official VA sources or accredited representatives.

Places veterans share experiences:

- Reddit r/Veterans
- Reddit r/VeteransBenefits
- Various Facebook groups
- HadIt.com forums

### What to Use Forums For

✅ Good for:
- Shared experiences
- Emotional support
- General guidance
- Finding resources

❌ Not for:
- Official VA policy
- Legal advice
- Medical advice
- Definitive answers
