### Veterans Crisis Line

| Method | Contact |
|--------|---------|
| **Phone** | 988 (then Press 1) |
| **Text** | 838255 |
| **Chat** | VeteransCrisisLine.net |
| **TTY** | 1-800-799-4889 |

**Available:** 24 hours a day, 7 days a week

For veterans in crisis or supporting someone in crisis. Confidential support from trained responders.

### Emergency Services

- **911** — Local emergency services
- **VA Emergency** — Contact your local VA Medical Center

## Specialized Lines

### Women Veterans Hotline

**1-855-829-6636**

Support specifically for women veterans:

- Healthcare questions
- Benefits information
- MST support resources
- Women-specific programs

**Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM ET; Saturday 8:00 AM - 6:30 PM ET

### Caregiver Support Line

**1-855-260-3274**

For veterans' caregivers:

- Caregiver program information
- Support services
- Resources and referrals

**Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM ET

### GI Bill Hotline

**1-888-442-4551**

Education benefits support:

- GI Bill questions
- School certification
- Payment issues
- Benefit status

**Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 7:00 PM ET

### Debt Management

**1-800-827-0648**

For VA debt questions:

- Payment plans
- Debt waivers
- Overpayment disputes
- Financial hardship

**Hours:** Monday-Friday, 7:30 AM - 7:00 PM ET

## Tips for Calling VA

!!! tip "Getting Through to the VA"
    
    1. **Call early** — Shorter wait times early morning
    2. **Have info ready** — SSN, VA file number, claim number
    3. **Be patient** — Hold times can be long
    4. **Take notes** — Document who you spoke with, date/time
    5. **Get names** — Ask for representative's name
    6. **Request confirmation** — Ask for confirmation numbers
    7. **Follow up in writing** — Important requests should be written
