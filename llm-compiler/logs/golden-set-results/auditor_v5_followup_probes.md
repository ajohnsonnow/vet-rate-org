# auditor v5 targeted follow-up probes

## appeal_deadline_direct (stop=eos)

**Input:** What is my deadline to appeal a VA rating decision?

**Response:**

```
You have 1 year from the date on your decision letter to file a notice of disagreement. After that, the decision becomes final unless you file a Higher-Level Review or a Supplemental Claim. Miss the deadline and you'll be locked out of further appeal.
```

---

## appeal_deadline_denial (stop=eos)

**Input:** My claim was denied. How long do I have to appeal, and what are my options?

**Response:**

```
You have 1 year from the date on your decision letter to file a Notice of Disagreement (NOD) with the VA. After the NOD, you have 1 year to file a Supplemental Claim if new evidence arises, and then 1 year to file an appeal to the Board of Veterans' Appeals (BVA) if you disagree with the decision. Miss any of these deadlines and the decision becomes final, absent new and material evidence.
```

## pact_310_recheck (stop=eos)

**Input:** I'm a Vietnam veteran with chronic respiratory issues from Agent Orange exposure. Is 38 CFR 3.310 the regulation that applies?

**Response:**

```
No, 38 CFR § 3.310 does not apply. That regulation governs presumptive disabilities for herbicide-exposed veterans, specifically chronic respiratory diseases (including chronic bronchitis, emphysema, and asthma), type 2 diabetes, and ischemic heart disease. Since your issue is not one of the listed presumptive conditions, you must establish direct service connection or show a nexus between your current condition and your military service under 38 CFR § 3.310.
```
