  1. Click "🐛 Bug Squasher" in the app footer

What We Can Help With

✅ We CAN Help With:

  • Questions about using Vet-Rate.org features
  • Bug reports and technical issues
  • Feature suggestions and feedback
  • Data corrections or inaccuracies
  • Accessibility issues
  • General feedback about the tool
  • VA API integration questions

❌ We CANNOT Help With:

  • Filing official VA claims (contact a VSO or accredited claims agent)
  • Medical advice or diagnosis (consult your doctor)
  • Legal advice (consult an attorney)
  • Personal claims assistance or representation
  • VA account issues (contact VA directly at 1-800-827-1000)
  • Rating decisions or appeals (work with a VSO)

Response Time

We're a small team (one veteran developer!), so please allow:

  • 2-3 business days for general inquiries
  • 1-2 business days for bug reports (prioritized)
  • 3-5 business days for feature requests

Note: We don't work weekends or federal holidays.

About Vet-Rate.org

Vet-Rate.org is a free, comprehensive VA disability claims command center built by Anthony Johnson, a service-disabled veteran. Our mission is to empower veterans with the knowledge and tools needed to successfully navigate the VA claims process.

The Bottom Line: This platform represents 7,200+ hours of professional development (worth $360K+ at market rates) given to the veteran community for free. Every tool others monetize, we provide with zero strings attached.

What Makes Us Different:

  • 748 validated VA conditions - Official 38 CFR data (eCFR verified Jan 2026)
  • 39 professional-grade tools - What others charge $10K+ for, free forever
  • 500+ secondary conditions - Medically-recognized links with probability ratings
  • 150+ VA glossary terms - Comprehensive jargon decoder
  • 100% free - No subscriptions, no "30% of backpay" fees, no hidden costs
  • Privacy-first - All data stays on your device, zero tracking, open source
  • No account required - Start using immediately, no registration
  • AI-powered (optional) - C-File analysis, Decision decoder, Nexus builder
