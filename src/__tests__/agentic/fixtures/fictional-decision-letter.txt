Veteran: Pat Q. Sample-Fictional

ISSUES
1. Service connection for ringing in the ears (tinnitus).
2. Service connection for a left knee strain.

DECISION
1. Service connection for tinnitus is granted with an evaluation of 10 percent.
2. Service connection for a left knee strain is denied.

EVIDENCE
- Service treatment records from the fictional Camp Placeholder clinic.
- A VA examination report for hearing and for the left knee.
- A written statement from the veteran.

REASONS FOR DECISION
1. Tinnitus. The examiner found it at least as likely as not that the
ringing in the ears began with noise exposure during service. Noise exposure
in service is conceded. Service connection is granted.

2. Left knee strain. The service treatment records show one visit for left
knee pain after a training march. The VA examination found a current left
knee strain. The examiner gave the opinion that the current strain is less
likely than not related to the visit in service, because the records show no
further knee complaints for the rest of service. No medical opinion in the
file links the current left knee strain to service. Service connection is
denied because the evidence does not show a link between the current
condition and service.
